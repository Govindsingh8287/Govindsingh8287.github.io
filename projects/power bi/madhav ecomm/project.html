Madhav Ecommerce Sales Power BI Dashboard

A profitability-focused sales dashboard tracking 5,615 units, ₹438K in revenue and ₹37K in profit across four states, five payment modes and three product categories. The report is designed to answer one question that revenue dashboards usually miss: which parts of the business actually make money, and which only look busy.

Dashboard Preview

Project Overview

Starting from transaction-level order and details tables, I modelled a star schema in Power BI, joining orders to order details on a shared key and adding a date table to support month and quarter analysis. DAX measures cover Quantity, Amount, Profit and Average Order Value, with quarter buttons (Qtr 1–4) and a category dropdown acting as global filters across the page. The visual design uses a dark theme with card KPIs at the top, a monthly profit column chart with conditional formatting that turns loss months red, and paired donut charts for category mix and payment behaviour. Sub-category and customer bar charts sit at the bottom to support drill-down into the specific products and buyers driving the totals
